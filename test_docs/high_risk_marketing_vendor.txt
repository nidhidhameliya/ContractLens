MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Termora Corp ("Client") and Global Marketing Dynamics LLC ("Agency"), effective as of October 1, 2026.

1. SERVICES
Agency agrees to provide digital marketing, SEO, and brand management services as described in the attached Statement of Work (SOW).

2. COMPENSATION
Client shall pay Agency a fixed retainer of $12,500 USD per month. Payments are due within 15 days of invoice receipt. 

3. TERM AND AUTO-RENEWAL
The initial term of this Agreement shall be 12 months. Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods. To prevent auto-renewal, Client must provide written notice of non-renewal no later than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the current term.

4. EARLY TERMINATION PENALTY
Client may not terminate this Agreement for convenience. If Client terminates this Agreement prior to the end of the term for any reason other than a material breach by Agency, Client shall immediately pay Agency the full remaining balance of the contract term plus a 30% early termination penalty.

5. LIABILITY
Under no circumstances shall Agency's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the total amount paid by Client to Agency in the one (1) month preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Client agrees to indemnify and hold Agency harmless from any claims, damages, or losses arising from the use of Agency's services, even if caused by Agency's negligence.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All marketing materials, campaigns, and concepts created by Agency shall remain the exclusive property of Agency. Client is granted a non-exclusive, revocable license to use the materials only during the term of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.
